Cookie policy

Last updated 14 November 2026

This site uses as little as possible on your device.

What we store

We remember your light or dark theme choice in your browser’s local storage, so the site looks the way you left it. It stays on your device and is never sent to us.

What we do not use

We do not use advertising or tracking cookies, and we do not run third-party analytics that follow you across sites.

Hosting

Our hosting and security provider may set strictly necessary cookies to protect the site from abuse. These are needed for the site to work and do not track you.

Your choices

You can clear local storage and cookies at any time in your browser settings. The site will still work; it will just forget your theme.
