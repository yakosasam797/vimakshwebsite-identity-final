# Vimaksh — Boil the Ocean.

Design direction and build specification for Claude Opus. Prepared 11 November 2026. This document specifies the site; it does not implement it.

## 1. The direction

**An expansive sea, followed by precise evidence.** Keep the final C4 hero intact. As the visitor leaves its coral water, the sea becomes six fine crimson contours on a light field. Those contours return at meaningful boundaries and in small interaction states. Large, tightly set Inter Tight, generous empty space, real project imagery, and a consistent right-edge navigation carry the rest of the experience.

Personality: **ambitious, exacting, curious, composed, tactile.** The boiling ocean expresses appetite and energy; the pages beneath it demonstrate care. The website should feel like the same studio at every depth, even when its background changes.

The defining contrast is between the hero's physical water and the rest of the site's controlled line drawing. Spend the dimensional spectacle in the hero. Do not add another 3D scene below it. Do not turn the site into a catalogue of ocean metaphors: no anchors, ships, nautical badges, thermometers, bubbles behind body copy, or invented depth readings.

## 2. What to learn from the references

### Brikken+Co

[Reference](https://www.brikken.co/?ref=unsection.com)

The page moves from a huge wordmark and immersive media into a large studio statement, an oversized work heading, two staggered portrait projects and a full-width project, a dark services sequence, studio credentials, questions, and a saturated contact/footer. A compact floating navigation appears over the long page. Warm pale ground, faint vertical grid lines, very large neutral sans typography, small mono navigation, and a recurring geometric mark make it coherent. Observed type ranged from approximately 20px work descriptions to a roughly 280px work heading; the studio statement was around 68px. Large gaps are part of the hierarchy. Scroll sequences focus attention on one idea at a time.

**Borrow:** the alternation between statement and evidence; images large enough to inspect; an occasional change of project scale; a footer that feels like a designed destination. Put relevant context beside each project instead of relying on a thumbnail gallery.

**Do not borrow:** its exact card arrangement, visible grid wallpaper, geometric symbol, claims about seniority, lengthy pinned services sequence, or portfolio content. Vimaksh currently has two evidenced experiments, not a large client portfolio. Its work section should be intentionally short.

### ++hellohello

[Reference](https://www.hellohello.is/?ref=unsection.com)

A dark dimensional plus-sign world opens the page. The story then covers the studio, overlapping human images, a client logo field, capability tabs and ruled service descriptions, four large project rows, recognition, location clocks, and a red-accent contact/footer. Project titles and context sit in a narrow left column; large image/video fields occupy the right. Compact navigation and a small plus mark stay near the top after the opening. Tight bold sans, restrained grey secondary text, thin rules, and repeated dimensional plus imagery bind the sections together. The work cursor offers a project action; videos and scroll staging make the work feel active.

**Borrow:** one identity device with several scales and jobs; persistent orientation; factual context next to expressive imagery; a human studio moment after the work. Keep the relationship of text and image consistent even when project colors differ.

**Do not borrow:** its plus sign, clock collection, music widget, overlapping collage, all-dark atmosphere, award count, or client-logo wall. Avoid carrying over its relatively low-contrast secondary text. Vimaksh's lighter hero needs a gradual handover and clearer reading surfaces.

### Noddal

[Reference](https://noddal.xyz/)

This is a continuous scene-led narrative rather than a studio case-study index. A loading arc introduces a floating compute structure in a blue landscape. Scroll travels through a luminous tunnel, code fragments, image-generation imagery, an eye/redacted chapter, and a branded portal before arriving at the creator's contact form/footer. Sparse large sans statements, small mono telemetry, stable corner chrome, and a left chapter queue give the changing scenes one visual grammar. Observed hero text was 80px/80px; supporting scene titles included 48px text. The same visual material and camera journey join chapters instead of unrelated transitions.

**Borrow:** continuity between the opening world and later graphic language; a limited set of persistent interface positions; transitions that explain a change of chapter. Vimaksh's sea-to-contour transition is the equivalent connective idea.

**Do not borrow:** its camera tunnel, sci-fi telemetry, simulated metrics, sound, long loading ritual, or scroll distance. Its final footer identifies übernatural and must not be mistaken for Vimaksh content. For a studio visitor, reading work and contacting the team must be faster than navigating a film.

## 5. Foundation: grid, type, spacing, color

At **1440px**, reserve 80px at the right for the vertical navigation. The content grid runs from x=48 to x=1312: **1264px**, 12 columns, 24px gutters, 83.333px columns. Left margin is 48px; the gap between content and the navigation rail is 48px. Column references below use inclusive spans; “1–4” occupies four columns. Full-bleed background fields and transition lines may cross the rail; text and project images may not. Cap this grid at 1264px on larger screens and center it within the area left of the 80px rail. At 1024–1439px use 32px left padding, the same rail, and 20px gutters.

Below **1024px**, remove the edge rail and use 24px page padding with a compact horizontal header. At 768–1023px use eight columns and 20px gutters; map the two desktop text/image regions to 3/5 columns. Below 768px stack them in reading order.

At **390px**, use a four-column grid: 20px margins, 12px gutters, 350px content width, 78.5px columns. Every reading block and project image spans all four columns. Metadata may use two columns per field. Never preserve a desktop offset as wasted mobile space. The hero keeps its supplied composition and mobile treatment. Test intermediate widths, 320px, and enlarged text; 390px is a target, not a minimum supported size.

All prose is left-aligned, normally 45–65 characters per line, maximum 72. No justified text. No global grid lines. Background boundaries are full width, with no rounded section containers.

## 8. The line-wave identity system

### Form and meaning

The supplied reference has thin crimson, nearly parallel waves on a white field. They braid at a few places, spread into a low swell, and leave most of the field empty. Preserve those qualities. The system below deliberately uses **six** principal contours to connect visually with the six-arrow mark; this is a new design relationship, not a claim about the original logo's symbolism.

Treat the graphic as a cross-section of moving water. “Heating” is expressed by slightly opening the spacing or amplitude in response to a visitor action. It never becomes flames, steam illustrations, a repeated sine-wave texture, a chart, or a pseudo-scientific measurement.

### Construction parameters, implementable in SVG or canvas

Prefer SVG for this small path count. Use no fill, round line caps and joins, and a **1 CSS-pixel stroke** at every viewport. Use non-scaling strokes. Draw beyond both horizontal edges by 24px and clip in the graphic's own band. Do not clip the page or the focus outline. Do not stretch a raster copy of the reference.

The canonical desktop macro band uses a 1440×160 coordinate field. Define normalized horizontal position `u = x / W`, line index `i = 0…N−1`, and centered line index `n = i − (N−1)/2`. The following is a mathematical drawing specification, not site code:

`y_i(u) = H/2 + A sin(2π(0.85u − 0.10) + φ + 0.045n) + n·s + n·B sin(2π(u + 0.08) + φ) + 1.5 sin(πu) sin(2πu + 0.28n)`

- Canonical `N=6`, `A=28px`, `s=4px`, `B=3.5px`, `φ=0`. The combination of offset phases and spacing creates gentle convergence and separation, including a small number of crossings. It must read as one long swell, not six independent oscillators.
- Sample every 8 CSS pixels desktop / 6 mobile. Interpolate smoothly with a cubic spline; keep identical sample positions on all lines. Avoid overshoot that creates cusps.
- Clamp every path to at least 12px inside its band's top/bottom bounds; if necessary reduce A, never clip a crest. Never change the band height during animation.
- Mobile macro: `W=390`, `H=88`, `A=12`, `s=2.5`, `B=2`, same low frequency and phase offsets. Use responsive parameters, not a horizontal crop of the desktop drawing.
- Keep the deterministic shape identical across refreshes. No noise seed, random jitter, dashed lines, glow, particles, or per-line color cycling.
- If the implementation makes crossings visually dense, reduce B by up to 0.5px; retain the six-line silhouette and broad spacing rhythm. Never add more paths to solve a weak composition.

### Scroll, hover, numbers, and restraint

For a macro band, use its passage from entering the viewport at the bottom to leaving at the top as progress `p` from 0 to 1. Let `φ` vary from −0.12 to +0.12 radians and A from 0.9× to 1.1× its base value. Clamp outside that interval. The change is tied to scroll, reversible, and confined to the graphic. It must not translate text or affect section positions. No idle loop below the hero.

On the active work image, frame lines appear with a 180ms opacity transition and spread from s=1.5 to s=2 over 320ms. On leave, reverse over 220ms. Do not animate path drawing around the whole image. Link underline morphs flat→low wave over 180ms; on touch it stays a straight visible underline. Do not add cursor trails or replace the native pointer. Functional loading uses one shared phase shift, with a 1.2s cycle only while a real request is pending; no fake percentage, full-screen overlay, minimum display time, or artificial loading delay. Show a retry/error state when the request fails.

Use numbers only when they identify an actual ordered process or a factual date. Current home sections are not numbered. Current projects do not get serial numbers disguised as achievement counts. If a future genuine process has steps, set its number in 12px mono with a 24px one-line leader; do not make numbers from wavy outlines. Source tags and dates stay normal text; the lines do not replace language.

**Color rule:** Crimson means the primary identity on a light field. Ink means structure on light, Sea Foam, or Coral. Tide Cyan means the same identity seen on Ink. No multicolor bundle. No Crimson-on-Coral fine line. No cyan fine lines on Sea Foam. No waves behind paragraphs, logos, navigation text, or project evidence.

**Density rule:** at most one macro line field in a normal viewport. Small active-link feedback may coexist. The quiet divider is the only exception within work. Never add a wave just because a blank area exists. Give major fields at least 96px desktop / 48px mobile clear distance from reading text. A 1px straight rule is still allowed for service rows and footer structure; not every line needs to be wavy.

All line graphics are decorative and hidden from assistive technology. Their absence must remove no meaning, state, link, or information.
