> About us

Vimaksh builds digital products with a distinct perspective and a practical approach. The company brings clarity to product decisions, care to design and depth to engineering, shaping each experience around the people who use it.

Custom software, and nothing else. From a Chrome extension to an industry-specific SaaS, AI automation, CRM or ERP: if your business runs on it, we build it.
